## 1. Cancellation flows: which save offers work, reported save rates, and offer ordering

### Takeaway
Reason-matched, multi-step cancel flows that lead with pause/skip/frequency-change and swap, and hold discounts to last, are the consensus best practice; reported save rates cluster at 15–35% (vendor-reported, named-brand cases 10–27%; Recharge's pause feature alone averages 9.6%). For a "too much product" consumable like fragrance refills, pause/skip and swap are the primary levers and a blanket discount is explicitly discouraged.

### Cited Findings
- Stay AI (vendor) says brands running its cancellation flows see 30–35% save rates; Loop reported a 9% save rate in one example from interrupting cancellation with subscription perks; general industry benchmark cited as 15–30% — [Loopwork: Top Shopify Subscription Apps 2026](https://www.loopwork.co/blog/best-shopify-subscription-apps) (Loop-authored) and [Stay AI: Best Shopify Subscription Management App](https://stay.ai/blog/best-shopify-subscription-management-app-dtc-brands/) (Stay-authored).
- A 3-step cancel-save flow with pause and discount offers "retains 25–35% of would-be churners" (aggregator claim, no primary data cited) — [Perspective AI: Best Cancellation Flow Software 2026](https://getperspective.ai/blog/best-subscription-cancellation-flow-software-2026-save-rate-reason-capture).
- Recharge (vendor): Cancellation Prevention "can save up to 33% of cancellations"; one merchant reached a 23% save rate via A/B testing the flow; a churn-survey modal lets customers pick a reason and then shows an incentive specific to that answer; merchants can mix incentives per reason and A/B test — [Recharge: Cancellation Prevention](https://getrecharge.com/products/retain/cancellation-prevention/); [Recharge Help: Getting started with Cancellation Prevention flows](https://support.getrecharge.com/hc/en-us/articles/14627290928919-Getting-started-with-Cancellation-Prevention-flows).
- Recharge (vendor) pause data: merchants using the pause feature see an average subscription save rate of 9.6% across 1,700+ active merchants; top Shopify merchants adding "pause before cancel" saw a 337% increase in pause adoption, preventing over 400,000 cancellations, with 3 of 4 pausers returning within months — [Recharge: Reduce Your Cancellations by 10% With Pause Subscriptions](https://getrecharge.com/blog/reduce-your-cancellations-by-10-with-pause-subscriptions/); pause/return figures also reported by [Ringly: Ecommerce subscription cancellation management](https://www.ringly.io/blog/ecommerce-subscription-cancellation-management).
- Stay AI named-brand cases (vendor case studies): OLIPOP had a 23.7% save rate and 14.7% MRR save rate in H1 2023 and cut active churn 26% after moving from Recharge; Lifeboost (coffee) built a treatment library and reached a save rate "over 27% and rising"; HOP WTR reached a 10% save rate using surveys plus automated treatments and cut churn 40% with flexible bundles; Kettle & Fire nets a 15% save rate with a humorous survey; A Pup Above saw >20% decrease in MoM churn — [Stay AI: Lifeboost case study](https://stay.ai/casestudy/lifeboost-retentionengine/); [Stay AI: HOP WTR case study](https://stay.ai/casestudy/hop-wtr-cuts-churn-with-stay/); [Stay AI: 17 RetentionEngine examples](https://stay.ai/blog/slashing-churn-with-retentionengine/); [Smile blog: Subscription loyalty programs (OLIPOP figures)](https://blog.smile.io/subscription-loyalty-programs-how-smile-and-stay-ai-keep-subscribers-from-canceling/).
- Loop named-brand cases (vendor): Four Sigmatic, with 8 re-engagement pages and 11 configured reasons, reached a 22% overall flow save rate as of early 2026; TruHeight's survey found taste/texture drove 30% of cancellations and after reformulating the flow held a 20.5% save rate; one API-integrated brand hit a 36% save rate; Loop says well-designed flows save 20–40%; adding video/visual content "consistently increases save rates by 5–10 percentage points"; Loop stresses 90-day retention of saved subscribers as the real metric — [Loopwork: Build a cancellation flow that saves 20–40%](https://www.loopwork.co/blog/shopify-subscription-cancellation-flow-save-churning-subscribers); [Loopwork: Best cancellation flows 2026](https://www.loopwork.co/blog/best-cancellation-flows-shopify-subscription-brands).
- Loop's flows are conditional multi-step journeys branching on order count, subscription duration, cancel reason and product type, with page-by-page save-rate analytics — [Loopwork: Loop Flows](https://www.loopwork.co/feature/loop-flows) (vendor).
- ProsperStack (vendor) markets Retain as preventing 10–39% of churn with reason-matched offers; Brightback (now Chargebee Retention) cases: Vital Proteins improved deflect rate 35% over a year via multivariate offer testing; Freshly improved save rates 50% in a year by personalizing offers on cancel reason, tenure, LTV, plan size — [ProsperStack: Retain](https://prosperstack.com/solutions/retain/); [Chargebee: Subscription ecommerce retention cheat sheet](https://www.chargebee.com/blog/subscription-commerce-customer-retention-insights/); [Perspective AI](https://getperspective.ai/blog/best-subscription-cancellation-flow-software-2026-save-rate-reason-capture).
- Offer ordering guidance: ask the reason first, then branch; pause is "almost always the better first offer" for temporary circumstances (with a claim that 70–80% of paused subscribers reactivate within 30 days, source unverified); a discount "should come after all four options, if it appears at all" because default discounts train customers to cancel for price cuts and erode perceived value; recommended order: pause, frequency/product change, downgrade, discount last — [Subscription Index: Cancellation flow guide](https://www.subscriptionindex.com/guides/cancellation-flow); [Recurflux: Cancel flow optimization](https://recurflux.com/resources/guides/subscription-cancel-flow-optimization-saas). Skio also publishes a cancel-flow best-practices guide — [Skio Help: Cancel Flow best practices](https://help.skio.com/docs/cancel-flow-best-practices-guide) (content not retrievable).
- Eli Weiss (operator): many cancel flows are "basically Mad Libs" showing the same 20% off regardless of reason; "if the customer has too much product, 20% off is just a cheaper way to make the problem worse" — offer pause or skip instead; always ask why before offering anything; reason-matched approaches save "up to 40%" per retention-platform data — [Eli Weiss: Subscription Retention and Churn Mitigation](https://www.eliweisss.com/p/subscription-retention-churn-mitigation); [Skio interview: The Eli Weiss Playbook](https://skio.com/interviews/the-eli-weiss-playbook-how-to-optimize-cx-and-retention).
- Consumer-side data: 27% of consumers would cancel if unable to pause or skip; 52% cancelled at least one subscription in the past year due to lack of use — [Swell: 40 Subscription Commerce Statistics](https://www.swell.is/content/subscription-commerce-statistics). Chargebee 2025 research: 78% of consumers demand pause or swap options — reported via [Loopwork: Complete guide to reducing subscription churn 2026](https://www.loopwork.co/blog/complete-guide-reducing-subscription-churn-shopify-2026).

### Inferences
- For a seven-scent, single-device brand where the likely top cancel reasons are "have too much" and "want variety," the flow should branch: too much → skip next / push date / change cadence to 45–60 days; bored of scent → one-click swap to another scent (with imagery); price → last resort, and preferably a non-cash offer (free sample vial, loyalty points) rather than a percentage cut, consistent with a no-discount premium posture.
- Realistic planning assumption: 10–20% save rate at launch, 20–30% after several months of reason-level testing. Treat vendor "30–35%" figures as ceilings from optimized, mature programs.

### Gaps
- No source broke out save rate by offer type (pause vs swap vs discount) for consumables; only Recharge's pause-only 9.6% is offer-specific.
- Could not retrieve Skio's cancel-flow best-practices page or Recharge's Cancellation Prevention analytics documentation to confirm defaults.
- No home-fragrance-specific cancel-flow data found.

## 2. Involuntary churn: dunning, account updater, pre-dunning, recovery rates, Shopify handling

### Takeaway
Involuntary churn is 20–40% of total churn; Shopify Payments' network card-account updater plus a subscription app's retry logic and Klaviyo-driven failed-payment email/SMS is the baseline, recovering roughly 50% of failures, with specialist tooling (Churn Buster) claiming 60–70%+ for top performers. Pre-dunning (30/14/7-day card-expiry reminders) is the cheapest incremental lever.

### Cited Findings
- Involuntary churn from failed payments accounts for up to 30% of total customer losses; recurring payment failure rates run 5–15% — [Easy Subscription: Payment recovery](https://easysubscription.io/glossary/payment-recovery/). Another source: involuntary churn is 20–40% of all churn; 30–50% of failed charges trace to an expiring or recently expired card — [Revenudge: What is pre-dunning](https://revenudge.com/blog/what-is-pre-dunning).
- Benchmarks for a healthy store: 95%+ first-attempt success, 50–70% recovery rate on failures, involuntary churn under 2% per month — [Loopwork: Why subscription payments fail on Shopify](https://www.loopwork.co/blog/why-subscription-payments-fail-on-shopify-and-how-to-fix-every-type) (Loop-authored).
- Shopify Payments' account updater automatically refreshes expired card details before the charge, "eliminating approximately 40% of failures silently"; card updater services generally prevent 30–40% of expiry-related failures — [Loopwork: Shopify subscription app for failed payment recovery](https://www.loopwork.co/blog/shopify-subscription-app-failed-payment-recovery); [Easy Subscription: Failed payment recovery](https://easysubscription.io/glossary/failed-payment-recovery/).
- Shopify Help Center: Shopify Payments works with major card networks to automatically update saved card details when customers get new cards; widely supported in the US; merchants can email a "Send link to update card" from the contract — [Shopify Help: Considerations and payment gateways for subscription products](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/products/purchase-options/subscriptions/considerations); [Shopify Help: Managing subscription contracts](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/products/purchase-options/subscriptions/shopify-subscriptions/manage-subscriptions/manage-contracts).
- Shopify's native Subscriptions app: in Settings > Billing attempts you can set number of retries, days between retries, and the action after all retries fail (skip, pause, or cancel); the retry logic is fixed-schedule with no decline-specific logic — [Shopify Help: Managing Shopify Subscriptions app settings](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/products/purchase-options/subscriptions/shopify-subscriptions/manage-subscriptions/manage-app-settings); characterization of "basic" logic from [Churn Buster: Smart dunning for Shopify 2026](https://churnbuster.io/articles/shopify-dunning) (vendor).
- Churn Buster (vendor): median recovery rate for its Shopify merchants ~50%, top performers 60–70%+; typical lift of 20%+ vs basic setups; positions subscription apps' dunning as "a checkbox feature" without decline-specific strategies or multi-channel recovery — [Churn Buster: Shopify dunning guide](https://churnbuster.io/articles/shopify-dunning); [Churn Buster: Shopify Subscriptions API](https://churnbuster.io/articles/shopify-subscriptions-api).
- Pre-dunning: emailing ~30 days before card expiry; customers receiving a 30/14/7-day reminder sequence update their card 60–70% of the time before the renewal attempt (blog claim, no primary dataset) — [Revenudge: What is pre-dunning](https://revenudge.com/blog/what-is-pre-dunning); "proper dunning recovers 70–85% vs 50–60% for default retry logic" (SaaS-oriented blog claim) — [Baremetrics: Dunning management guide](https://baremetrics.com/blog/ultimate-dunning-management-guide).
- Recharge sends a "Charge Failed" metric to Klaviyo so failed-payment messaging can run in Klaviyo instead of Recharge's native email — [Recharge Help: Using Recharge subscription event metrics as Klaviyo triggers](https://support.getrecharge.com/hc/en-us/articles/9828306941207-Using-Recharge-subscription-event-metrics-as-triggers-for-Klaviyo-email-and-SMS-flows); ZHS describes pairing Recharge dunning with Klaviyo messaging — [ZHS Ecom: Subscription failed-payment recovery](https://www.zhs-ecom.com/resources/subscription-failed-payment-recovery).
- Recharge Win Backs now include a payment-method-update feature, so customers who churned for invalid payment can add a card directly on the win-back landing page (Recharge advises removing the Klaviyo filter that excluded those cancel reasons) — [Recharge Help: Win Backs through Klaviyo](https://support.getrecharge.com/hc/en-us/articles/28565831828247-How-to-create-a-Win-Backs-Through-Klaviyo).
- Loop and Recharge quick-action links let subscribers update payment method without logging in from the upcoming-order or failed-payment email — [Klaviyo Marketplace: Loop Subscriptions](https://marketplace.klaviyo.com/en-us/apps/01HVRHQ1E9960PPZK6BPV8W5X1/); [Recharge Help: Quick Action URLs](https://support.getrecharge.com/hc/en-us/articles/360052553513-Building-and-testing-Quick-Action-URLs-to-skip-delay-swap-and-access-the-customer-portal).
- Ordergroove launched an "Involuntary Churn Suite" — [Ordergroove blog](https://www.ordergroove.com/blog/introducing-ordergroove-involuntary-churn-suite/) (details not retrievable).

### Inferences
- At Maison Croyez's launch scale, Shopify Payments account updater + the chosen app's built-in retries (e.g., 3–4 attempts spaced 2–3 days) + a Klaviyo "Charge Failed" flow (email day 0, SMS day 1 with a passwordless update-card link, email day 3, day 6) should reach the ~50% recovery band; Churn Buster is a later add-on once failed-payment volume justifies its fee.
- Because refills are $-modest monthly charges, soft declines (insufficient funds) dominate; retry timing around paydays (1st/15th) is worth testing, but no Shopify-specific data on that was found.

### Gaps
- Could not retrieve exact default retry schedules for Recharge, Skio, Loop, Stay AI, or Appstle, nor Churn Buster's Shopify pricing.
- The "40% of failures eliminated silently" account-updater claim is from a Loop blog, not Shopify; Shopify publishes no recovery-rate figure.
- No consumables-specific dunning recovery data (all figures are cross-industry).

## 3. Onboarding and the first 90 days

### Takeaway
Churn is concentrated at orders 1–3, not month six; Skio reports 70–80% of supplement subscribers churn before order 3 while the best brands keep 50–60% past order 3. Operators (Weiss, Skio) argue onboarding, usage education, and delivery timing matter more than the cancel flow; a first-cancel-attempt time under 60 days signals broken onboarding. Hard data quantifying onboarding's lift on 90-day retention was not found.

### Cited Findings
- Skio (June 22, 2026): most supplement subscribers quit at order 2; roughly 70–80% churn before order 3; best brands keep 50–60% past order 3 with proactive retention; time to first cancel attempt under 60 days "means your onboarding is broken"; playbook includes cadence flexibility (30/45/60/90 days), using order history to proactively suggest adjustments, and rewarding order milestones 3, 6, 12 to shift the frame from spend to tenure — [Skio: Why 70% of Supplement Subscribers Churn After Order 2](https://skio.com/blog/why-70-of-supplement-subscribers-churn-after-order-2). Recharge has a parallel piece — [Recharge: Supplement subscription retention, a leaky first reorder](https://getrecharge.com/blog/supplement-subscription-retention/) (not retrievable).
- "Subscribers experience the highest churn at orders one, two, and three, not month six"; cohort curves flatten around order 3–5 and that is where intervention beats blanket discounts — attributed to Skio via [Finsi: Best Shopify subscription apps 2026](https://www.finsi.ai/blog/best-shopify-subscription-apps-2026/).
- Eli Weiss (July 16, 2026): five levers ahead of the cancel portal — onboarding that sets expectations, usage education, delivery timing that prevents backstock, failed-payment handling, and support moments; "most subscription retention problems are expectation problems rather than billing problems" — [Eli Weiss: Five Ways to Actually Fix Subscription Churn](https://www.eliweisss.com/p/five-ways-to-actually-fix-subscription).
- Weiss also recommends surprise-and-delight at milestones (small freebie at 6 months, handwritten note at order #10) as low-cost perceived-value builders — [Eli Weiss: Subscription Retention and Churn Mitigation](https://www.eliweisss.com/p/subscription-retention-churn-mitigation).
- Non-subscription DTC benchmarks: best-in-class brands convert 25–30% of first-time buyers to a second order within 90 days (most under 20%); 50% of second orders occur within 30 days and 76% within 90 days; a good 90-day repeat rate for consumables is 30–45% — [Everboost: What is a good repeat purchase rate](https://everboost.co.uk/insights/what-is-a-good-repeat-purchase-rate/); [Taylor Sicard: DTC retention benchmarks 2026](https://taylorsicard.com/blog/dtc-retention-benchmarks-2026).
- Onboarding tactics with an evaluation method rather than results: package inserts routing to setup content, first-name welcome video, QR code to a usage guide, measured via 30/60/90-day repeat for onboarded vs non-onboarded cohorts — [Top Growth Marketing: 90-day retention guide](https://topgrowthmarketing.com/ecommerce-customer-retention-90-day-guide/).
- Recharge's Surprise & Delight flow adds a free gift or discount to an upcoming order via a sequential template; free gifts are one-time products and do not create a free subscription; a Klaviyo node can notify the customer, and up to four custom event properties can be passed; A/B tests can be segmented in Klaviyo by who did not receive an incentive — [Recharge Help: Building a Surprise and Delight flow](https://support.getrecharge.com/hc/en-us/articles/13753682375063-Building-a-Surprise-and-Delight-Subscription-Experience). Skio has an equivalent Surprise & Delight rule with notification guidance — [Skio Help: Surprise & Delight](https://help.skio.com/hc/en-us/articles/16802750121243-Surprise-Delight).
- Recharge merchant-base retention curve: 45% of subscribers still active at 6 months, 33% at 12 months — attributed to Recharge's State of Subscription Commerce via [SubJolt: Churn rate benchmarks](https://www.subjolt.com/guides/churn-rate-benchmarks/). Replenishment models retain ~45% at 12 months vs meal kits under 15% — [Rivo: Lifestyle ecommerce retention statistics](https://www.rivo.io/blog/lifestyle-ecommerce-retention-statistics).

### Inferences
- For a diffuser refill, the analogous "product hasn't worked yet" risk is the customer not having fully used the first refill when order 2 ships (stockpiling), and the diffuser not being placed/used optimally. An onboarding sequence should cover device placement, intensity settings, expected refill life at each setting, and an explicit "here's when your next refill ships and how to move it" message before order 2.
- A day-20 to day-25 "how's your first scent lasting?" touch with one-tap push/skip/swap links directly addresses the order-2 cliff.

### Gaps
- No study found that quantifies the 90-day retention lift from onboarding sequences or early surprise gifts for consumable subscriptions; claims are directional.
- Could not retrieve Skio's or Recharge's supplement-retention posts in full to confirm the cohort methodology behind the 70–80% figure.

## 4. Cadence and quantity management: customer-set cadence, usage reminders, proactive skip, stockpiling

### Takeaway
Overstock is repeatedly cited as the number-one voluntary churn reason for consumables; Ordergroove (vendor) claims AI-predicted proactive skip prompts cut churn by up to 17% and that subscribers who skip stay 135% longer; Recharge (vendor) says pause-before-cancel prevents ~10% of cancellations. Letting customers set cadence at checkout and nudging cadence changes from order history is the standard recommendation.

### Cited Findings
- Ordergroove (vendor): overstock "continues to be the number one reason for subscriber churn"; Anticipate AI identifies subscribers at overstock risk and messages them to skip, which "can reduce churn by as much as 17%"; subscribers who can skip "remain in the fold for 135% longer" — [Ordergroove: Stop losing subscribers to overstock](https://www.ordergroove.com/blog/stop-losing-subscribers-to-overstock-proven-retention-strategies-from-leading-brands/); [Ordergroove: Subscriber churn guide](https://www.ordergroove.com/blog/understanding-subscriber-churn/). Anticipate AI churn prediction is exposed via API — [Ordergroove developer docs](https://developer.ordergroove.com/v2.6.0/reference/anticipate_ai_v1_churn_prediction_list).
- Swanky (agency) describes Ordergroove's overstock prediction plus personalized skip prompts and frames proactive delay invitations as protecting LTV — [Swanky: Subscription retention strategies](https://swankyagency.com/subscription-retention-strategies-reduce-churn-increase-ltv/).
- Loop (vendor): mismatched default frequency causes accumulation, "one of the top cancellation reasons"; two consecutive skips signal disengagement and should trigger outreach offering a different frequency; surfacing skip proactively ("Got too much? You can skip anytime") makes subscribers less likely to cancel outright; Loop claims pause/skip/swap flexibility cuts churn "by up to 40%" — [Loopwork: Pause, skip, or swap playbook](https://www.loopwork.co/blog/subscription-flexibility-pause-skip-swap-reduce-shopify-churn).
- Recharge (vendor): pause feature yields a 9.6% average save rate; blog title claims cancellations reduced by 10%; merchants can see who pauses, for how long, and which products — [Recharge: Reduce Your Cancellations by 10% With Pause Subscriptions](https://getrecharge.com/blog/reduce-your-cancellations-by-10-with-pause-subscriptions/).
- Churn warning sign (blog, unverified methodology): customers who modify frequency more than twice are 65% more likely to cancel within the next billing cycle — [Recurringo: Warning signs your customers will cancel](https://recurringo.com/blog/how-to-spot-churn-before-it-happens).
- Skio's supplement playbook recommends offering 30/45/60/90-day cadences and using order history to suggest adjustments proactively — [Skio: Why 70% churn after order 2](https://skio.com/blog/why-70-of-supplement-subscribers-churn-after-order-2).
- Swap: the ability to swap to a new flavor/variant "keeps the subscription alive while satisfying desire for change"; curated-feeling subscriptions retain 2–3x better than generic (Loop claim, no dataset) — [Loopwork: Complete guide to reducing churn 2026](https://www.loopwork.co/blog/complete-guide-reducing-subscription-churn-shopify-2026).
- Recharge, Skio and Loop all support passwordless quick-action links (skip, delay, swap, add item, update payment) inside Klaviyo emails/SMS — [Recharge Help: Quick Action URLs](https://support.getrecharge.com/hc/en-us/articles/360052553513-Building-and-testing-Quick-Action-URLs-to-skip-delay-swap-and-access-the-customer-portal); [Skio on Klaviyo Tech Partner Directory](https://connect.klaviyo.com/integrations/skio); [Klaviyo Marketplace: Loop Subscriptions](https://marketplace.klaviyo.com/en-us/apps/01HVRHQ1E9960PPZK6BPV8W5X1/).

### Inferences
- A 30-day default cadence for a single refill is a stockpiling risk if actual burn is 5–7 weeks at typical intensity settings. Offering 30/45/60-day cadences at checkout and a "usage check" prompt before order 2 and order 3 ("Still have more than half? Push your next refill two weeks") is the highest-leverage, lowest-cost tactic here, and it can be implemented in Klaviyo with the "Upcoming Charge" event plus quick-action links without any AI feature.
- Track skip frequency as a health metric: two consecutive skips should trigger a cadence-change offer, not a discount.

### Gaps
- Ordergroove's 17% and 135% figures are vendor claims without disclosed methodology; Ordergroove is also priced out of range (see Section 9), so the tactic must be replicated manually.
- No usage-based (sensor/consumption) reminder data found for fragrance devices; no data on average diffuser refill burn rates.

## 5. Loyalty and milestones: points, subscriber tiers, milestone rewards, referrals, premium-brand fit

### Takeaway
Points-on-recurring-orders with redemption against the next refill, a subscriber-only VIP tier, and non-cash milestone rewards (order 3/6/12) are the standard stack; Smile, LoyaltyLion, Yotpo and Rivo all integrate with the major subscription apps. Reported ROI is aggregator-level (4.8x average, 5–15% LTV lift, tiered programs 1.8x higher ROI) rather than subscription-specific; premium brands are advised to reward with access, experiences, and product rather than discounts.

### Cited Findings
- Smile (vendor): a subscription loyalty program combines points that accumulate the longer a customer stays subscribed, a VIP tier reflecting relationship length and value, and rewards that make skipping or cancelling "feel like it comes with a cost"; Smile + Recharge auto-awards points on recurring orders and lets customers redeem points for discounts on upcoming subscription orders; VIP entry rewards and tier-specific earning conditions are supported; points balance and tier sync to email — [Smile: Recharge integration](https://smile.io/integrations/recharge); [Smile blog: Subscription loyalty programs with Stay AI](https://blog.smile.io/subscription-loyalty-programs-how-smile-and-stay-ai-keep-subscribers-from-canceling/); [Smile Help: VIP rewards and perks](https://help.smile.io/en/articles/4036320-vip-rewards-and-perks).
- Smile supports 90,000+ merchants incl. 4,423 on Shopify Plus; positioning: Smile best for $500K–$10M brands, Yotpo Loyalty for $2M–$50M brands already on Yotpo Reviews, LoyaltyLion for $10M+ enterprise customization — [Smile: Smile vs Yotpo](https://smile.io/learn/smile-vs-yotpo) (vendor); [Evolve: Smile vs Yotpo vs LoyaltyLion 2026](https://evolveamz.com/ecommerce-loyalty-smile-yotpo-loyaltylion-2026/). Yotpo sunset three major products in 2025 and narrowed focus around Reviews — [Yotpo: Yotpo vs LoyaltyLion vs Smile](https://www.yotpo.com/blog/yotpo-vs-loyaltylion-vs-smileio/) (vendor).
- Aggregate ROI claims (Yotpo/Rivo content, no primary study): 90% of programs report positive returns averaging 4.8x; top performers 15–25% annual revenue lift; tiered structures deliver 1.8x higher ROI, VIP members 73% higher AOV and 3.6x more purchases — [Yotpo: Effectiveness of loyalty programs](https://www.yotpo.com/blog/effectiveness-of-loyalty-programs-a-guide-to-data-roi/); [Rivo: Loyalty program statistics](https://www.rivo.io/blog/loyalty-program-statistics). Another aggregator: 5–15% LTV lift, 15–30% repeat-rate lift, 5–12% AOV lift among members; enrollment target 25–40% of customers within 90 days — [Evolve 2026 comparison](https://evolveamz.com/ecommerce-loyalty-smile-yotpo-loyaltylion-2026/). Loyalty members generate 12–18% more incremental revenue annually — [Rivo: Lifestyle retention statistics](https://www.rivo.io/blog/lifestyle-ecommerce-retention-statistics).
- Milestones: Skio recommends rewarding order 3, 6, 12 to reframe the relationship around tenure — [Skio: order-2 churn post](https://skio.com/blog/why-70-of-supplement-subscribers-churn-after-order-2); Weiss recommends a freebie at 6 months and a handwritten note at order #10 — [Eli Weiss](https://www.eliweisss.com/p/subscription-retention-churn-mitigation).
- Premium-brand structure: luxury programs emphasize access, recognition and experiences over points-for-discounts; Sephora Beauty Insider's Rouge tier ($1,000 annual spend) unlocks free shipping, early access, priority on limited editions and events, with Rouge retention reported around 90% and the program driving an estimated 80% of revenue; Louis Vuitton runs unadvertised VIP access; Misha Nonoo uses status tiers with styling and event rewards — [Gameball: Best luxury loyalty programs](https://www.gameball.co/blog/the-best-luxury-loyalty-programs-how-to-create-one-that-works); [Antavo: Luxury fashion loyalty programs](https://antavo.com/blog/luxury-fashion-loyalty-programs/); [BLOY: Luxury loyalty without discounts](https://bloy.io/blog/luxury-loyalty-programs-how-premium-brands-build-loyalty-without-discounts/); Smile's own piece on experiential rewards — [Smile blog: 5 experiential rewards for luxury brands](https://blog.smile.io/5-examples-of-experiential-rewards-for-luxury-brands/).

### Inferences
- Structure that fits "never discount hardware": points earned per refill redeemable only for refills, sample vials, or accessories (never the diffuser); a subscriber-only tier granting early access to new scents, a free discovery vial with order 3, a full free refill at order 6 or 12, and an annual "anniversary" gift; referral reward paid in refill credit or a scent-discovery set rather than cash off. Smile (Shopify-native, Recharge/Skio integrations, SMB pricing) is the practical default; LoyaltyLion if deeper tier customization is needed later.
- Because scent choice is the core variable, "swap-plus-tier" beats "points-plus-discount": the retention value of exclusive scent access is high and margin-neutral.

## 6. Prepaid and membership structures

### Takeaway
Annual/prepaid plans show materially lower churn than monthly in every dataset cited (40–51% lower churn; 96% vs 70% 12-month NRR for supplements on annual vs monthly; 28% vs 11% retained at month 12), largely by removing 11 monthly decision points and failed-payment opportunities, and they front-load cash. Typical prepaid discounts are 15–25% for annual commitments. Evidence on "pay upfront, get a free item" and paid membership fees is thin (one anecdote: a $5/month membership produced ~1.5x LTV).

### Cited Findings
- Swell's 2026 subscription-box synthesis (from Recharge and Bold platform data) puts churn 51% lower on annual vs monthly; another cited figure is 40% lower churn for annual subscribers — [Eightx: Churn by billing period 2026](https://eightx.co/blog/average-ecommerce-subscription-churn-by-billing-period-2026); [Swell: Subscription commerce statistics](https://www.swell.is/content/subscription-commerce-statistics).
- Annual prepay subscribers retain 2.5x longer than monthly (28% vs 11% at month 12); mechanism: pre-commitment removes the monthly decision point and eliminates eleven additional failed-payment opportunities — [Eightx: Churn by billing period 2026](https://eightx.co/blog/average-ecommerce-subscription-churn-by-billing-period-2026).
- Supplements on annual billing hold ~96% net revenue retention at 12 months vs 70% on monthly (26-point gap); "annual billing is the single biggest retention lever" — [Eightx: Subscription revenue as % of DTC sales](https://eightx.co/blog/average-subscription-of-revenue-for-dtc).
- Most effective prepaid strategies offer 15–25% discounts for annual commitments; prepaid secures commitment and immediate cash flow — [SmartSMSSolutions: Subscriptions in 2026, prepaid, dunning, save offers](https://smartsmssolutions.com/resources/blog/business/article-1-hub-subscriptions-prepaid-dunning-save-offers) (secondary).
- Prepaid plans deliver a lump sum to reinvest and customers who paid for 6 months "are far less likely to cancel before their plan period ends" — [Easy Subscription: Prepaid plans guide](https://easysubscription.io/glossary/prepaid-plans/) (vendor).
- Skio supports prepaid subscriptions and prepaid gift subscriptions where the recipient gets their own login and control — [Skio Help: Prepaid subscriptions](https://help.skio.com/docs/getting-started-with-prepaid-subscriptions); [Skio Help: Prepaid gift subscriptions](https://help.skio.com/docs/how-to-set-up-prepaid-gift-subscriptions). Stay AI: only prepaid selling plans can be configured as gifts — [Stay AI Help: Gifting subscriptions](https://help.stay.ai/en/articles/8178638-gifting-subscriptions). Recharge notes prepaid gift subscriptions show as churned in analytics at term end — [Recharge Help: Prepaid gift subscriptions in analytics](https://support.getrecharge.com/hc/en-us/articles/21192974020503-Why-do-my-prepaid-gift-subscriptions-show-as-churned-in-my-analytics).
- Paid membership anecdote: one brand's $5/month membership produced members with ~1.5x the LTV of non-members; paying members "have a dramatically higher commitment level" — [EmberTribe: Customer loyalty program guide 2026](https://embertribe.com/blog/customer-loyalty-program) (secondary, brand unnamed).

### Inferences
- A 3/6/12-refill prepaid option priced at or slightly better than the 25% monthly discount, with a non-cash sweetener (e.g., a free discovery set or an exclusive scent) rather than deeper percentage discounts, fits the premium positioning and shifts cash forward. The "free item" should never be the diffuser to protect hardware pricing.
- Prepaid must still honor swap-anytime, or the variety promise breaks; confirm the chosen app supports swaps within prepaid terms (Skio and Stay AI document prepaid; details on mid-term swaps not confirmed).

### Gaps
- No named case study quantifying the churn or LTV effect of "pay upfront, get a free item" offers.
- No DTC data on membership-fee models beyond the unnamed $5/month anecdote; Amazon Prime-style fee models were not researched for this brand size.
- The month-12 retention split (28% vs 11%) comes from an Eightx blog whose underlying dataset could not be inspected.

## 7. Communication cadence, legal renewal notices, SMS vs email, Klaviyo flow architecture

### Takeaway
Send the upcoming-charge notice 3–5 days ahead (app defaults ~3 days; one benchmark argues 7–10 days for monthly), include renewal date, amount, and a one-click cancel/skip/swap path; that also satisfies the direction of state auto-renewal laws (California, Minnesota, Connecticut now require at least annual reminders regardless of term; the FTC click-to-cancel rule was vacated July 8, 2025 but ROSCA and state laws still apply). Klaviyo flow SMS clicks ~10% vs email flow clicks ~5.6%, so SMS with passwordless quick-action links is the better channel for time-sensitive renewal and failed-payment messages. Recharge, Skio and Loop all push lifecycle events into Klaviyo; Recharge ships prebuilt Klaviyo flow templates.

### Cited Findings
- Recharge's default upcoming-charge notification goes 3 days before the charge and is adjustable; "Order upcoming on ReCharge" can replace it as a Klaviyo trigger; a "Consented to SMS?" conditional split routes SMS vs email — [Recharge Help: Quick Actions with Klaviyo flows](https://support.getrecharge.com/hc/en-us/articles/4405551381143-Recharge-and-Klaviyo-use-cases); [Recharge Help: Notifications](https://support.getrecharge.com/hc/en-us/articles/360006356293-Notifications).
- Skio best practice: send upcoming-order reminders 3–5 days before charge; Skio email timing/content is customizable or routable through Klaviyo — [Skio Help: Subscription best practices](https://help.skio.com/docs/subscription-best-practices); [Skio Help: Email notifications](https://help.skio.com/docs/skio-email-notifications). Counterpoint benchmark: monthly subscribers respond best to reminders 7–10 days out; earlier than 10 days raises unsubscribes without improving retention — [MailerToGo: Renewal email timing benchmarks](https://resources.mailertogo.com/statistics/subscription-renewal-email-timing-benchmarks) (unverified dataset).

### Inferences
- Recommended Klaviyo architecture for the refill program (all triggers available from Recharge, Skio, or Loop):
  1. Subscription Started → onboarding series (day 0 welcome/expectations, day 2 device setup and intensity guide, day 7 "scent stories," day 20 usage check with push/skip/swap links).
  2. Order Upcoming (T-3 to T-5 days) → email plus SMS to consented profiles; content: ship date, amount, scent, one-tap skip/swap/push; annual variant for California/Minnesota/Connecticut compliance if the app's native notice is turned off.
  3. Charge Failed → day 0 email, day 1 SMS with passwordless update-card link, day 3 email, day 6 final; suppress on Charge Paid.
  4. Subscription Skipped → single "was it too much?" email offering cadence change; second consecutive skip → cadence-change offer plus note from founder.
  5. Subscription Swapped → confirmation plus tips for the new scent (reinforces variety).
  6. Subscription Cancelled → reason-branched exit email; win-back at days 7/30/60/90 (see Section 8).
  7. Milestones (order 3/6/12 via order-count property) → surprise-and-delight gift notifications.
- Turn off the app's native emails for any event Klaviyo handles to avoid duplicates; keep the app's transactional fallback for legally required notices if Klaviyo deliverability is ever in question.

## 8. Win-back: offers, timing, reactivation rates

### Takeaway
Consensus cadence is day 7 (catch accidental/payment cancels), day 30 (offer), day 60 optional, day 90 last call; a 4-touch, reason-segmented sequence reactivates roughly 10–18% of cancelled subscribers versus ~6% for a single email, and former subscribers now account for about 20–25% of new subscription sign-ups industry-wide (Recurly).

### Cited Findings
- A single-email win-back reactivates ~6% of lapsed subscribers; a 4-email sequence 14–18% (attributed to a Klaviyo 2025 benchmark) — [Mailmend: Win-back campaign statistics](https://mailmend.io/blogs/win-back-campaign-statistics) (secondary; original Klaviyo source not located).
- Standard cadence: Day 7, Day 30, Day 90, with optional Day 14 and Day 60 for higher-ARPU; by Day 30 "the easy reactivations are gone"; beyond 3–4 touches deliverability suffers; a 4-email sequence at days 7/30/60/90 segmented by cancel reason reactivates 10–15% at roughly one-fifth the CAC of cold acquisition — [Subscription Index: Win-back campaigns](https://www.subscriptionindex.com/guides/win-back-campaigns).
- Healthy ecommerce win-back rate 5–15%; strong campaigns 10–30% — [Alexander Jarvis: Subscriber win-back rate](https://www.alexanderjarvis.com/what-is-subscriber-win-back-rate-in-ecommerce/); [Top Growth Marketing: Winback campaign 2026](https://topgrowthmarketing.com/ecommerce-winback-campaign-recover-lapsed-customers-in-2026/).
- Recurly 2026 State of Subscriptions (2,200 merchants, 76M subscribers): former subscribers drive nearly 1 in 4 new sign-ups; ~20% of new acquisitions in 2025 were returning subscribers — [Recurly: State of Subscriptions report](https://recurly.com/content/state-of-subscriptions-report/); [Recurly: Customer winback strategies](https://recurly.com/blog/customer-winback-strategies-for-subscriptions/).
- Automated win-back emails average 42.51% open, 18.27% CTR, 10.34% conversion, outperforming promotional emails by 460% on conversions (aggregator claim) — [Mailmend](https://mailmend.io/blogs/win-back-campaign-statistics).
- Recharge Win Backs: reactivation landing page shows the cancelled product, the applied offer (example "15% off for the next 5 cycles"), new price and expiry; can be targeted by cancel reason; includes payment-method update for invalid-card churn — [Recharge Help: Win-Back campaigns with incentives](https://support.getrecharge.com/hc/en-us/articles/24154171473303-Creating-Win-Back-campaigns-with-incentives-to-reactivate-subscribers); [Recharge Help: Target by cancelled subscription](https://support.getrecharge.com/hc/en-us/articles/28304600725783-How-to-target-customers-based-on-cancelled-subscriptions-when-building-Win-Back-flows); [Recharge: Churn prevention through win backs](https://getrecharge.com/subscriptions/churn-prevention/win-backs/).
- Recharge blog lists four win-back strategies (content not retrievable) — [Recharge: Re-engaging inactive subscribers](https://getrecharge.com/blog/re-engaging-inactive-subscribers-4-win-back-strategies-to-explore/).

### Inferences
- For fragrance refills the natural win-back hook is a new scent launch or seasonal scent rather than a discount: "too much product" cancellers should be re-approached when their stock would plausibly be depleted (60–90 days), while "wanted variety" cancellers should get new-scent announcements immediately. Segment the win-back by cancel reason captured in the flow.
- If a monetary offer is used, a time-boxed multi-cycle offer (Recharge's "X% off next N cycles" pattern) beats a permanent discount and can be replaced by a free discovery vial to stay on-brand.

### Gaps
- No consumables-specific reactivation rates by offer type; the 14–18% figure's original Klaviyo source could not be verified.
- No data on whether new-product-launch win-backs outperform discount win-backs.

### Inferences
- Fit ranking for Maison Croyez at launch (premium, one device, seven scents, swap-anytime, Klaviyo-centric, no app yet):
  1. Loop Starter ($99 + 1.0%): lowest all-in cost among full-featured apps, month-to-month, reason-branched cancel flows with analytics on the base tier, Klaviyo events plus passwordless quick actions, free tier for a soft launch. Watch-out: not a Klaviyo Customer Hub subscription provider yet.
  2. Recharge Standard ($99 + 1.25% + $0.19, or $25 to 50 subscribers): most mature Klaviyo integration (prebuilt flow templates, Quick Actions, Customer Hub), gifting included, Win Backs and Surprise & Delight built in; higher per-order fee; Skio's tech may fold in over time.
  3. Skio Scale ($599 + 1% + $0.20): best-in-class passwordless portal, Quick Actions, Klaviyo Helpdesk/Customer Hub, churn-risk events; expensive at launch volume and its roadmap is now Recharge's.
  4. Appstle (≤$100, 0%): cheapest viable path with swaps, build-a-box, retries and Klaviyo events; less polished portal and cancel-flow depth per third-party reviews.
  5. Stay AI, Ordergroove: deferred until subscription revenue justifies $500–$3,000/month minimums.
  6. Shopify native: free but lacks Klaviyo lifecycle events and swap/cancel-flow sophistication; acceptable only as a stopgap.
- Build-a-box is low priority for a single-refill program; swap UX and scent-discovery gifting matter more, which favors apps with one-click swap links in email/SMS (Recharge, Skio, Loop).
