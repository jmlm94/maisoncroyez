Strategy notes

The seven strategies in this flow

  1. Flexibility before cancel. Skip, swap, timing and pause sit above the cancel link, since up to 19% of cancels come from people who did not know they could adjust.
  2. Ask permission to help. One screen asks whether she wants to see options. Cancel now is right beside it. Minnesota now limits save offers shown without consent.
  3. Match the fix to the reason. Too much product gets skip or slower timing. Price gets slower timing or a smaller bottle. Never a coupon.
  4. Explain nose adaptation. Two weeks of constant scent dulls the nose. She thinks it stopped working. The fix is rotation and intermittent timing, not a refund.
  5. Fix before cancel. A device problem routes to the warranty, with billing paused until it is solved.
  6. Money only as fragrance. The single monetary save is a free 50ml in her next box. No percentage discount, no diffuser discount.
  7. A graceful exit. After cancelling she gets one-time options: her subscriber price on one more bottle, a low-bottle reminder with no plan attached, and an open door with a gift if she returns.
