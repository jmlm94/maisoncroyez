01/The brand

Made for the evening.

Maison Croyez gives the end of the day back to the woman who spent it on everyone else. Its scented rituals help her relax, come back to the moment, and sleep. They are simple to choose, simple to use, and finished to feel more expensive than they are.

Mission

Give her the evening back.

Make switching off simple. One scent and one small ritual, as easy as 1, 2, 3.

Audience

Women 35 and up.

She gives all day to work, family and everyone else. At night she wants one small thing that works, and she does not want a project. Every page also stays easy to read for the woman of 55 and over who buys today.

Personality

Calm and plain-spoken.

Warm, assured, a little wry. Never mystical and never loud. Maison talks like a friend who has her evenings figured out.

Tagline · proposed
Believe in the evening.

Croyez is French for believe. The line says what the name means and when the brand shows up. It replaces “Attract what you sense”.

Two alternates
The day ends here.Plain and final. Strong on night sections.
Stress, meet your match.The line you liked. Best as a campaign headline.

Brand pillars

01

Evening first.

Everything is made for the hours after the day is done. Night is our time, our light and our mood.

02

Scent leads.

Mood arrives through scent. Relaxing leads to being present, and being present leads to sleep.

03

Simple as 1, 2, 3.

Few choices, few steps, few words. If something needs explaining twice, it gets cut.

04

High-end, within reach.

It looks and feels premium. It never sounds precious and it never shouts.

Voice & tone

Short. Plain. Benefit first, with a light smile. Maison Croyez sounds like a calm friend, never a guru and never a salesperson.

French touch

“Bonsoir” opens and “Bonne nuit” closes. Two words are enough.

Emojis

Rare. About one message in seven, one at a time, never in headlines or buttons.

Claims

Feelings and body language are fine. No medical or certified claims until a document backs them.

02/Logo

The mark we keep.

The logo is the one thing that carries over. All four versions stay exactly as they are, redrawn here as vectors from the 2023 files.

Two-line wordmark · primary

The default. Footer, social avatars and anywhere with room.

One-line wordmark

The site header and any space that is short on height.

Stamp

Monogram with the name. Formal and square formats.

Monogram

Favicon, app icon, seals and patterns. Upright or on its side.

Rules

Clear space
The height of the wordmark’s M on every side.
Minimum width
Two-line 96 px · one-line 140 px · stamp 72 px · monogram 24 px.
Color
Night on light grounds. Cream or white on Night, on the signature color and on photos. Nothing else.
Never
Stretch it, recolor it, outline it, add effects or retype it in another font.

The one-line version is rebuilt from the two-line letters because no original file of it exists. Every version is traced from the 1080 px originals, which is right for screens. Print will need a hand-finished master.

04/Typography

Type system.

Compare the headline style

Ketro’s two families, with one change. DM Sans leads in bold, and the mono moves down to labels, where it adds precision without making the brand read like a lab.

Licensing & fallbackEvery font on this board is free and open-source, served by Google Fonts. There is nothing to buy or renew. Fallback stacks: DM Sans, system-ui, Arial, sans-serif · Roboto Mono, ui-monospace, Menlo, monospace.

05/Imagery

Art direction.

The hour the day lets go.

Maison Croyez pictures live at dusk and after. One warm lamp, deep soft shadows and a trace of blue at the window. Real women of 35 and over, caught mid-exhale and never posing. Around them there is water, steam, linen and plaster.

Light

Grade

DoShoot it like this

  • Keep it dusk or later, and protect the shadows.
  • Use one warm light, plus the blue of the window.
  • Show real skin, real age and a real home.
  • Leave empty space where the words will go.

Don’tAvoid this

  • Bright, white, midday bathrooms.
  • Models smiling at the camera.
  • Crystals, altars or anything mystical.
  • Busy flat lays and rainbow color.

Every picture on this board is a generated reference for the direction. None of them is final photography.

06/Iconography

Marks & elements.

Soft line icons on a 24-unit grid.

Icons are thin lines with round ends and no fills, drawn to sit beside DM Sans. An icon explains something or it is left out. The site uses the open-source Lucide library with the stroke set to 1.5 px.
