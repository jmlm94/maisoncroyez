Does 50 / 30 / 20 make sense?

Yes. The three fit together because each one does a different job.

Ketro · 50%
The frame. Off-white and black, one accent colour, a big confident wordmark, proof near the top, short sentences.
Mozi · 30%

Reference 02 / Cleared

Eight contradictions, now cleared

Your ruling: where two things contradict, both go. Every item here is blank in the new brand until we rebuild it. The live pages stay as they are until you say otherwise.

  1. Four colour systems at once.Toolkit blush and gradients, the per-scent boxes, the store’s pink-to-coral bar, and the landing pages’ black, gold and green.
  2. Two voices.Quiet luxury in the documents, hard-selling health and savings on the pages that bring the orders.
  3. Rules against reality.The brand bible (Project Heart, May 2026) forbids free-diffuser offers, medical-sounding claims and emojis. The live pages use all three.
  4. The intentions disagree.Golden Blossom Harmony is “Love” on the box, “Peace” in Project Heart and “Prosperity & Harmony” in the character bible.
  5. The category rule.Project Heart defines Maison as a home fragrance brand for women 30 and up, and rules out candles, room sprays and perfume. Shower steamers, bath soaks and creams take the brand to the body.
  6. The name.“Maison” means house. It fits a diffuser. Whether it fits a body cream is your call.
  7. Origin claims conflict.One guide says “100% organic ingredients from France,” another says never claim “Made in France,” and Project Heart names a U.S. supplier.
  8. Missing source files.The toolkit PDF in the repo lost its images: the logo versions, palette, patterns and packaging renders are blank. There is no vector logo.

What all four share

  1. One owned colour on calm neutrals. Olive, coral, lime, teal. Maison has no single colour today.
  2. A type voice you would recognise without the logo.
  3. Packaging is the hero of every picture, and it is colour-coded.
  4. Product names in plain words: a benefit, or a scent you can imagine.
  5. Proof near the top: review counts, press, experts, short “no ___” lists.
  6. Few products and clear first offers: sets, trios, a sample kit, a quiz.

They differ in temperature. Where Maison should sit on this line is the first question.

Reference 04 / The board to build

The final document, in the Carbinox format

The Carbinox board has six numbered parts. Maison needs the same six, plus three it does not show.

  • CoverBrand guidelinesOne-line description, version, source.
  • 01The brandStatement, mission, audience, personality, four pillars, voice and tone.
  • 02ColourCore colours, neutrals, 70 / 20 / 10 ratio, contrast checks.
  • 03TypographyFamilies, licences and fallbacks, the full type scale.
  • 04ImageryArt direction, light and grade, treatments, do and do not.
  • 05Marks and elementsIcon grid, stroke, the core icon set.
  • 06UI and touchpointsButtons, chips, ratings, FAQ rows, navigation, sample pages on desktop and phone.
  • + MaisonLogo systemWordmark, monogram, clear space, minimum sizes.
  • + MaisonScent systemHow each scent is named and coloured across every product.
  • + MaisonPackagingDiffuser, oils, shower steamers, bath soaks, creams.
