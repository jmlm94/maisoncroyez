FAQ

Questions? Answered.

Is this better than plug-ins, candles and room sprays?{{i:plus}}

Plug-ins fade within a week, a candle scents the four feet around the flame for an hour, and a spray lasts a few minutes. This runs on pure fragrance oil as a fine dry mist, fills a room in about ten minutes and keeps going all day.

Are the scents really free?{{i:plus}}

Yes. Every diffuser comes with one 100 ml scent of your choice, free, whether or not you want refills.

How do refills work?{{i:plus}}

Say yes in step 3 and your scents come again every 4 weeks for $39 each, with an email reminder before every one. Skip a delivery, swap scents or cancel any time. Say no and nothing renews.

Will it grow mold or leak like my last one?{{i:plus}}

No. There is no water in it: no tank, no standing water, nothing that can grow. The bottle locks into the diffuser, so nothing spills and there is nothing to clean.

How many diffusers do I need?{{i:plus}}

One covers up to 600 square feet: an open-plan main floor or a large bedroom. For a two-storey home most customers run two, one near the entrance and one upstairs.

What is in the scents?{{i:plus}}

Each 100 ml bottle is pure fragrance oil built on notes you can name:

  • Golden Blossom Harmony: buttercup, honeysuckle and sunflower.
  • Crisp Citrus Scape: yuzu leaf, green mandarin and cypress.
  • Chilled Citrus: chilled lavender, eucalyptus and white citrus.
  • Honey Nectar: ginger milk, white birch and eucalyptus honey.
  • Euphoric Bloom: jasmine tea, white peach and sandalwood crème.
  • Wildwood Mystique: huckleberry, wild juniper and mountain fern.
  • Midnight Sensation: moonflower, night lily and skin musk.
What if I don’t like it?{{i:plus}}

Live with it for 30 days. If your home doesn’t feel different, send the diffuser and the scents back with our prepaid label and we refund everything. The diffuser itself is covered for life.
