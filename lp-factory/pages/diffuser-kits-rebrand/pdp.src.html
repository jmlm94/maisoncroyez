Questions? Answered.

Will it help me switch off in the evening?{{i:plus}}

Not by itself, and we won’t pretend it does. It gives the evening a signal: one press when you walk in, and the same scent every night. If your evenings don’t feel different in 30 days, we refund every dollar.

Can I leave it on when I go to bed?{{i:plus}}

Yes. There is no flame, no water and no heat. Set the timer for 1, 3, 6 or 12 hours and it stops on its own. The night light switches on or off separately.

I don’t know how the scents smell. What if I pick wrong?{{i:plus}}

Each scent is described by three notes you would recognize, and your first ones are free. If you don’t love one, we swap it, free.

Is it too strong? I’m sensitive to scent.{{i:plus}}

There are three strengths, and the lowest is soft. Crisp Citrus Scape and Chilled Citrus are the two lightest scents. If one is too much for you, we swap it, free.

Am I signing up for a subscription?{{i:plus}}

Only if you want one. In step 3 you pick refills every 30, 60 or 90 days, or no refills at all. With refills, your scents come again for $29.95 each instead of $39.95, and we email you before every charge. Skip a delivery, swap scents or cancel any time. Today’s scents are free either way.

What if I don’t like it?{{i:plus}}

Live with it for 30 days. If your evenings don’t feel different, tell us. We send a prepaid label and refund you right away, even if you’ve tried the scents. The diffuser itself is covered for life: if it ever stops working, we replace it.

Is it safe around kids and pets?{{i:plus}}

There is no flame, no smoke, no heat and no water, so it is safe to leave on overnight, in a kid’s room or near pets. Start on the subtle setting in the room where your pet sleeps.

Someone at home has allergies. Is it a good fit?{{i:plus}}

Nothing burns and nothing is sprayed, and the oils are hypoallergenic. If someone is sensitive to fragrance, start on the subtle setting. You have 30 days to send everything back for a full refund.

How long does one bottle last?{{i:plus}}

About 15 to 21 days on the everyday setting. Higher settings use it faster.

How long does delivery take?{{i:plus}}

About 5 business days. Every order ships free from Miami.

How big is the diffuser?{{i:plus}}

4 x 4 inches wide and 11.5 inches tall (103 x 103 x 293 mm), and it weighs 2.25 pounds. About the footprint of a coffee mug, so it fits on a nightstand, a shelf or a counter.

Will it grow mold or leak like my last one?{{i:plus}}

No. There is no water in it: no tank, no standing water, nothing that can grow. The bottle locks into the diffuser, so nothing spills and there is nothing to clean.

How many diffusers do I need?{{i:plus}}

One covers up to 600 square feet: an open-plan main floor or a large bedroom. Two cover where you sit and where you sleep. For a two-storey home most customers run two, one near the entrance and one upstairs.

What is in the scents?{{i:plus}}

Each 100 ml bottle is pure fragrance oil built on notes you can name:

  • Golden Blossom Harmony: buttercup, honeysuckle and sunflower.
  • Crisp Citrus Scape: yuzu leaf, green mandarin and cypress.
  • Chilled Citrus: chilled lavender, eucalyptus and white citrus.
  • Honey Nectar: ginger milk, white birch and eucalyptus honey.
  • Euphoric Bloom: jasmine tea, white peach and sandalwood crème.
  • Wildwood Mystique: huckleberry, wild juniper and mountain fern.
  • Midnight Sensation: moonflower, night lily and skin musk.
Is this better than plug-ins, candles and room sprays?{{i:plus}}

Plug-ins fade within a week, a candle scents the four feet around the flame for an hour, and a spray lasts a few minutes. This runs on pure fragrance oil as a fine dry mist, fills a room in about ten minutes and keeps going all day.
