Sales, ad results, the September survey, real reviews, the brand documents, the site data and what competitors do. Each part says where it came from. Nothing was changed on the store or the ad accounts.

What this means for the quiz.

  1. The top reason people almost didn’t buy is “I don’t know how it smells.”From the September survey. Price was second, the subscription third. Answering the first one is the quiz’s whole job.
  2. She is older than the brand’s target.83% of ad purchases come from women 45 and over, 65% from women 55 and over. The quiz needs large type, few taps and no jargon.
  3. Nine in ten visits are on a phone, most inside Facebook or Instagram.The typical visitor scrolls about a quarter of a long page. The quiz has to be short and start high on the page.
  4. Three scents carry the store: Golden Blossom Harmony, Midnight Sensation, Euphoric Bloom.59% of all scent bottles sold. The pages currently mark Crisp Citrus Scape as a best seller; by units it is fifth.
  5. Customers come back, and many stay on one scent.56% ordered more than once. Of those with three or more scent orders, 43% never changed scent. The first match matters.
  6. They buy scents in sets, and Midnight Sensation is in most of them.The quiz should end with one, two or three scents that go together, matched to the number of diffusers.
  7. Nearly all came from candles, plug-ins, sprays or a water diffuser.“What do you use today?” is a natural question, and it tells us which worry to answer (mold, flame, strength).
  8. Nobody has described the scents by nose.Strength, mood and “smells like” were written from the ingredient list. Before the quiz recommends anything, Patricia or you should check the scent sheet below.

What they ask before buying.

Your own question lists and the kits page, in the order people hesitate

  1. I don’t know how the scents smell. What if I pick wrong?
  2. Is it too strong? I’m sensitive to scent. Will I actually smell it, or does it fade?
  3. Am I signing up for a subscription?
  4. Is it safe around kids and pets? Someone at home has allergies.
  5. Can I leave it on when I go to bed?
  6. Will it grow mold or leak like my last one?
  7. Is this better than plug-ins, candles and room sprays?
  8. How many diffusers do I need? How long does a bottle last?
  9. Does it remove pet and food smells?

Rules the quiz has to follow.

  • No intentions, manifestation or “attract”. Relaxation leads: unwind, be present, rest.
  • Feelings are fine. No medical or certified claims (sleep, anxiety, allergy) without a document.
  • Short and plain, benefit first, a calm friend. “Bonsoir” opens, “Bonne nuit” closes.
  • No invented reviews or numbers. No Amazon or competitor names.
  • Your layout rules: no small labels above titles, no numbered labels, no middle dot, compact, tiles the same size, real photos.
  • Your ruling from the builder: don’t jump people forward automatically, “people need to choose carefully.”
