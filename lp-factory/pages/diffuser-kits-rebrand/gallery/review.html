Maison Croyez | Waterless diffuser

Seven pictures, seven answers.

Square, 2000 x 2000, for the product page gallery. Each picture answers one question a first-time visitor has before she buys, in the order the customer survey and the page’s own questions put them. Every layout is different and none carries a number. To save a picture, press and hold it.

01 | Text column and product panel

What is it, and will I smell it?

Cold visitors do not know what a waterless diffuser is. The three numbers are the facts the current page already states.

02 | Dark list with the seven boxes

I can’t smell it through a screen.

The first reason buyers gave in the customer survey for almost not buying. Answered with the free scent, the seven scents with their notes from the printed boxes, and the free swap.

“One free scent with every diffuser” is the offer on this preview page. Change the line if the offer changes.

03 | Photo with one card

Can I leave it on and go to bed?

Bedroom use came up on its own in the survey. The quote is a real answer from it, about the diffuser.

The bedroom picture is generated from the real product photo.

04 | Comparison table

Why not just a candle or a plug-in?

The comparison she is already making. Same four rows as the slide on the page.

05 | Three photos and a band

Is it one more thing to look after?

One of the four worries on the page. Three steps, the three strengths, the timer and the night light.

Strength names (Subtle, Balanced, Full) come from the brand kit’s G1, G2, G3.

06 | Big number on a color block

What does it cost me after today? Am I locked in?

Price and the subscription were reasons two and three in the survey.

“Under $2 an evening” assumes a $29 refill that lasts 15 days. Remove the seal if the refill price changes.

07 | Color block with an arch photo

What if I don’t like it?

The last thing between her and the button: the 30 days, the prepaid label, the scent swap, the warranty.
