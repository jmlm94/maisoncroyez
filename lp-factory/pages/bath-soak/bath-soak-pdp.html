What’s inside (the honest label)

250 g of magnesium chloride flakes, essential oil for the scent and a little jojoba oil. Nothing else. Unwind is sandalwood, Be here is eucalyptus and Drift off is lavender. It is a bath soak, not a supplement.

How to use it

Run the water comfortably warm, about 104 to 108°F. Pour in the whole bag and swirl once. Soak for 15 minutes, 1 to 2 hours before you want to be asleep. For a foot soak, use half a bag in a basin of warm water.

FAQ

Questions? Answered.

How do they smell? What if I don’t like one?

Unwind is sandalwood: warm and woody. Be here is eucalyptus: fresh and clear. Drift off is lavender: soft and floral. The sampler has one of each. If you don’t love a scent, we swap it, free.

I don’t have a bathtub. Can I still use it?

Yes. Use a foot basin: half a bag in warm water, 15 minutes.

How hot, how long, and when?

Comfortably warm, about 104 to 108°F. Fifteen minutes. One to two hours before you want to be asleep.

Does the magnesium go through my skin?

We don’t claim that. Research doesn’t support the idea that a bath raises your magnesium levels. What is supported: a warm bath 1 to 2 hours before bed is linked to falling asleep sooner. The flakes and the scent make it a ritual you keep.

Is it OK for sensitive skin, or if I’m pregnant?

It is made with magnesium chloride flakes, essential oil and a little jojoba oil. If you have sensitive skin, are pregnant or have a health condition, check with your doctor first.

How many baths are in a bag?

One. A bag holds 250 g: one full bath, or two foot soaks.

How does the subscription work?

You get 15% off every delivery, every 30, 45 or 60 days. We email you before every charge. Skip, swap scents or cancel any time. You can also just buy once.
