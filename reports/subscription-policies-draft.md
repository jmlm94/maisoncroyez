## 1. Subscription Terms

Last updated: [date]

These Subscription Terms apply when you start a Maison Croyez fragrance subscription. They form part of our Terms of Service. By starting a subscription you agree to them. Where these terms and the rest of the Terms of Service differ, these terms apply to your subscription.

### 1.1 What the subscription is

A Maison Croyez subscription delivers 100ml fragrance bottles to you on a recurring schedule. You choose your scent and your delivery frequency, and you can change both at any time as described below.

When you buy a Maison Croyez diffuser kit and start a subscription in the same order, the fragrance in that first order is free. The recurring price for each bottle after that is the subscriber price shown at checkout, currently $39.95 per 100ml bottle (regular price $49.95). Shipping is free on every subscription delivery.

### 1.2 Delivery frequency

You choose deliveries every 30 days, every 45 days or every 60 days. You can change your frequency at any time from your subscription page. A change applies from your next scheduled charge.

### 1.3 How and when you are charged

Your payment method on file is charged on each scheduled charge date, and your bottle ships within a few days of that charge. We email you at least 3 days before every charge with the amount, the date, the scent, and one-click links to skip, swap, change timing, pause or cancel. Charges continue on your schedule until you cancel, subject to the minimum commitment in section 1.5.

### 1.4 Skip, swap, pause and change

From your subscription page at [maisoncroyez.com/pages/manage-subscription] you can, at any time and without contacting us:

- Skip your next delivery. Your schedule moves forward by one cycle. Nothing is charged for a skipped delivery.
- Swap your scent. Choose any fragrance in our range for your next delivery at the same price.
- Change your delivery frequency between 30, 45 and 60 days.
- Pause your subscription for up to 90 days in total. Nothing ships and nothing is charged while paused. Your subscription resumes automatically at the end of the pause period you choose, and we email you 5 days before it resumes so you can extend the pause (within the 90-day limit), resume early, or cancel.

Changes made less than 3 days before a scheduled charge may apply to the following charge instead, because the order may already be processing.

### 1.5 Minimum commitment and diffuser ownership

Because the fragrance in your first order is free and the kit price is set with an ongoing subscription in mind, a new subscription carries a minimum commitment of three recurring fragrance charges after your first purchase. On the 30-day schedule that is about 90 days. Skipped deliveries and paused time do not count toward the three charges; only completed charges do.

Once your three recurring charges are complete, the diffuser or diffusers from your kit are fully yours with nothing further owed, and you can cancel at any time with no conditions.

If you want to cancel before the three charges are complete, you choose one of the following on your subscription page:

- Return the diffuser(s). Send back each diffuser from your kit in like-new condition with its original packaging and accessories, using tracked shipping at your cost, within 14 days of your cancellation request. Your subscription ends the day you request cancellation, and nothing further is charged once we confirm your return has shipped. Fragrance you have received is yours to keep.
- Keep the diffuser(s). Pay a one-time diffuser fee of $49.95 for each diffuser in your kit. We charge this fee to your payment method only after you confirm this choice on your subscription page. Your subscription ends immediately and nothing further is charged.
- Complete the remaining charges. Continue your schedule, or skip ahead and take the remaining charges sooner. When the third recurring charge is complete, the diffuser(s) are yours and your subscription ends if you have asked it to.

Buying a one-time fragrance supply package (see section 1.8) also satisfies the minimum commitment.

If you return your entire first order under the 30-Day Money Back Guarantee (see the Refund and Return Policy), your subscription is cancelled with nothing further owed and the minimum commitment does not apply.

### 1.6 How to cancel

You can cancel online at any time at [maisoncroyez.com/pages/manage-subscription] using the email address on your order. Cancellation takes effect right away for any future charge. An order that has already been charged will still ship. You can also cancel by emailing info@maisoncroyez.com with the subject line CANCEL and your order number; we process email cancellations within one business day and confirm by email.

If your subscription is inside its minimum commitment, you will be asked to choose one of the options in section 1.5 as part of cancelling. We do not require a phone call, a chat, or a reason to cancel, and we never keep charging after you have cancelled.

### 1.7 Price changes

We may change the subscriber price, but only for future charges and only after emailing you at least 30 days in advance. If you do not want to continue at the new price you can cancel before it takes effect, subject to section 1.5.

### 1.8 One-time supply packages

From your subscription page you can replace your subscription with a one-time fragrance supply package (for example a 3-month, 6-month or 12-month supply). A supply package is charged once, ships once, and does not renew. Buying one closes your subscription and satisfies the minimum commitment in section 1.5. Any diffusers included with a supply package are yours with nothing further owed.

### 1.9 Failed payments

If a scheduled charge fails, we will email you and retry the charge over the following days. If we cannot collect payment after those attempts, we will pause your subscription and let you know. You can update your payment method at any time on your subscription page.

### 1.10 Your records and reminders

We email a confirmation of these terms, your schedule and your price when you start your subscription, and again whenever you change them. At least once a year we will email you a reminder of your subscription terms, your renewal schedule, and how to cancel.

### 1.11 Your rights

Nothing in these terms limits the rights you have under the laws of your state, including any automatic renewal law that applies where you live.

## 2. Refund and Return Policy

Last updated: [date]

At Maison Croyez, every diffuser and every scent is crafted to elevate your home. If something is not right, this page explains exactly what we will do about it.

### 30-Day Money Back Guarantee

Every first order is covered by a 30-day money back guarantee, counted from the delivery date confirmed by carrier tracking. To be eligible:

- Request your return within 30 calendar days of delivery by emailing info@maisoncroyez.com with the subject line RETURN and your order number. We reply with return approval and instructions. Unapproved packages cannot be processed.
- Diffusers must be in like-new condition, free of damage, with all original packaging and accessories.
- Scent oils must be sealed and unopened. Opened or used oils are final sale and cannot be returned or refunded. The free fragrance included with a kit does not need to be returned.
- Return shipping is arranged and paid by the customer using a tracked service. We are not responsible for return packages lost in transit without valid tracking.
- Ship your return within 7 business days of approval. Once we receive and inspect it, your refund goes to your original payment method within 5 to 10 business days.

Kits and bundles: if the scent has been opened or used, the refund applies to the diffuser only. If the scent is sealed, both are refundable. If a returned package arrives incomplete, the value of the missing items is deducted from your refund.

If your first order started a subscription and you return the full order under this guarantee, your subscription is cancelled at the same time with nothing further owed.

### Subscription deliveries

Recurring fragrance deliveries are not covered by the 30-day guarantee, since you can skip, swap, pause or cancel before any charge. Every charge is announced by email at least 3 days ahead with one-click links to make changes. If a recurring bottle arrives damaged or leaking, or you received the wrong scent, email us within 14 days with a photo and we will send a replacement at no cost.

For cancellation rules, the minimum commitment, and what happens with your diffuser if you cancel early, see the Subscription Terms.

### One-Year Limited Warranty [extended while subscribed]

Every Maison Croyez diffuser is covered by a one-year limited warranty against manufacturing defects, counted from the delivery date. [For as long as your fragrance subscription remains active, this warranty is extended and continues to cover your diffuser beyond the first year.]

What is covered: electrical or mechanical failure under normal use, manufacturing and material defects.

What is not covered: damage from drops, spills, misuse or improper cleaning; use of oils not intended for your diffuser; normal wear; lost, stolen or discarded units.

Warranty claims are resolved through replacement, not refund. Email info@maisoncroyez.com with your order number and a photo or video of the issue. If approved, we ship your replacement within 3 to 5 business days of receiving the defective unit. If you are a subscriber, your next charge is paused from the day you open a claim until your replacement is working. Each product is eligible for one warranty replacement per order.

### Order cancellations and changes

One-time orders can be cancelled or changed within 1 hour of purchase by emailing info@maisoncroyez.com with the subject line CANCEL and your order number. After 1 hour, orders enter fulfillment and can no longer be cancelled. Subscriptions can be cancelled at any time as described in the Subscription Terms.

### Lost, refused and returned-to-sender packages

If tracking shows delivered but you cannot find your package, check with household members and neighbors, then contact us within 7 days so we can open a carrier investigation and resolve it. If a package is refused or returned to sender, you are eligible for a free reshipment or a full refund once it arrives back at our facility.

### How to reach us

Email info@maisoncroyez.com. We answer within one business day.
