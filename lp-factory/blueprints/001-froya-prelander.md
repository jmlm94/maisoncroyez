# Blueprint 001 — Frøya-style Pre-Lander
Source: Frøya Organics mobile scroll capture (48pp PDF). Output: `/pages/maison-croyez-landing-page`. Cold FB/IG traffic → PDP click-through. All CTAs → PDP, label + ` ➔`.

## Final section order (as shipped, after iteration)
announcement → hero (bg image + logo inside, no header bar) → transformations (3 cards, carousel) → howTo (3 steps, autoplay videos) → intentionMap (7 gradient scent cards, rituals visible) → ctaBreak feature (gradient band, big line) → stats (gradient bars) → offer1 (image·title·value prop·price·CTA·trust3) → founder letter → guarantee (90-day badge) → ugc (3 stacked testimonials) → offer2 (= offer1) → ladder (5 struck-through bad rungs + featured MC card) → ctaBreak → faq (10 Qs) → guarantee compact. No footer (owner choice), body bottom-padded for sticky bar.

## What iteration taught us (apply to future pages by default)
- Hero: compact spacing; logo in banner; sub ~2-3 lines; NO proof bullets (deleted); CTA sub "*Free 100ml fragrance included."
- Emphasis phrase gradient purple→orange; every headline punctuated; letter-spacing −5%; leading −15%.
- Kill: press logos, review wall, benefits grid, no-BS grid, modes section, email capture, values, origin (owner trimmed aggressively toward shorter page).
- Offer blocks: identical twice; simple (no kit selector on pre-landers — selection happens on PDP).
- Ladder pattern is a keeper: competitors struck-through + ✕, MC as gradient featured card.
- Sticky bar: single full-width gradient pill, no price.

## Frøya structural DNA (reuse for any Frøya-like reference)
Proof-dense top (stars+count above headline) → mechanism story → interactive centerpiece ("their Arctic plants" = our Intention Map) → repeated buy-box → founder → social proof wall → guarantee badge → FAQ. Loud claims translated to quiet-luxury register; their orange CTA = our black pill.
