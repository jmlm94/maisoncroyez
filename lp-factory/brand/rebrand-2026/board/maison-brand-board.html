01/The brand

Made for the evening.

Maison Croyez gives the end of the day back to the woman who spent it on everyone else. Its scented rituals help her relax, come back to the moment, and sleep. They are simple to choose, simple to use, and finished to feel more expensive than they are.

Mission

Give her the evening back.

Make switching off simple. One scent and one small ritual, as easy as 1, 2, 3.

Audience

Women 35 and up.

She gives all day to work, family and everyone else. At night she wants one small thing that works, and she does not want a project. Every page also stays easy to read for the woman of 55 and over who buys today.

Personality

Calm and plain-spoken.

Warm, assured, a little wry. Never mystical and never loud. Maison talks like a friend who has her evenings figured out.

Tagline · decided
Exhale the day.

Scent is breath, and this is the breath out. Three words say what Maison is for, and they hold for every scent and every room.

Where
Under the logo in the footer, at the end of emails and ads, and as the closing line of the home page.
How
Always these three words, with the period. Never reworded, never a button label.
Headlines
“Stress, meet your match.” and “Leave the day at the door.” stay in use as campaign headlines.

“Bonsoir” opens and “Bonne nuit” closes. Two words are enough.

Emojis

Rare. About one message in seven, one at a time, never in headlines or buttons.

Claims

Feelings and body language are fine. No medical or certified claims until a document backs them.

The height of the wordmark’s M on every side.

Header: centered, about 104 px wide, with the menu on its left and the icons on its right. Footer: 160 px.

Browser tab icon · the one exception

A browser tab icon is 16 px square, and two lines of lettering cannot be read at that size. In that single spot the M of the wordmark stands alone. Everywhere else it is the full two-line logo.

Pattern

The M, tone on tone

The M of the wordmark repeated quietly, one shade off its background. It is a texture and never a second logo. Use it for gift moments, an email header or the footer band, and never behind paragraphs.

The vector is traced from the 1080 px original, which is right for screens. Print will need a hand-finished master.

Color beyond the signature

Every other color comes from the pictures and the scents, never from the page itself. Each scent brings one soft tint, used only on its own tile. The three shown here are placeholders until the scents are chosen. A seasonal campaign may bring one guest color and a pattern into the hero and the top bar, and the rest of the page stays as it is.

04/Typography

Type system.

Bold sans is confirmed. DM Sans leads in bold and Roboto Mono handles the labels. These are Ketro’s two families, with the mono moved from headlines down to labels.

Licensing & fallbackBoth fonts are free and open-source, served by Google Fonts. There is nothing to buy or renew. Fallback stacks: DM Sans, system-ui, Arial, sans-serif · Roboto Mono, ui-monospace, Menlo, monospace.

Display / headlines

DM Sans

Bold, round and modern. It shares the geometry of the logo and stays calm at large sizes. Always sentence case, closed with a period.

05/Imagery

The hour the day lets go.

Maison Croyez pictures live at dusk and after. One warm lamp, deep soft shadows and a trace of blue at the window. Real women from 35 to 70 and over, styled with care, caught mid-exhale and never posing. Around them there is water, steam, linen and plaster.

DoShoot it like this

  • Keep it dusk or later, and protect the shadows.
  • Use one warm light, plus the blue of the window.
  • Cast across ages, silver hair included. Real skin, real homes.
  • Leave empty space where the words will go.

Don’tAvoid this

  • Bright, white, midday bathrooms.
  • Models smiling at the camera.
  • Crystals, altars or anything mystical.
  • Busy flat lays and rainbow color.

Every picture on this board is a generated reference for the direction. None of them is final photography.

06/Iconography

Marks & elements.

Soft line icons on a 24-unit grid.

Icons are thin lines with round ends and no fills, drawn to sit beside DM Sans. An icon explains something or it is left out. The site uses the open-source Lucide library with the stroke set to 1.5 px.
