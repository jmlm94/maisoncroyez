# Maison Croyez brand and business context (internal, from repo documents and live store data)

Source: "Project Heart" brand bible v2.0 (May 2026), Creative Brief (July 2026), Creator Guide, plus live Shopify and Klaviyo reads on 2026-09-20. Treat this file as ground truth for brand facts; the other notes in this folder are external research.

## What the business is
- Luxury home fragrance brand. Waterless cold-air plug-in diffuser plus seven intention-based 100ml scents. Sold to women 30+ (core 35 to 48), US, homeowners or invested renters, wellness-oriented, "home as sanctuary."
- Positioning: "attract what you sense." Each scent maps to an intention (love, peace, clarity, abundance, purification, energy, manifestation). Quiet luxury aesthetic. Founder and face: Patricia. CEO: Jose Lepage (also CEO of Carbinox).
- Strategic bet #2 in the bible: "Subscription is the business. The diffuser is acquisition. The scents are the business."
- Competitors named: Aroma360, Hotel Collection, Pura, Aera, Aromatech, Drift, Scentiment.

## Product and economics (bible, May 2026)
- Diffuser: 120ml reservoir, three modes (G1 10s on/220s off, G2 20s/120s, G3 25s/60s), timers 1/3/6/12h, up to 600 sq ft, plug-in, zero maintenance, 1-year warranty. Bible price $89.95, COGS $23. Live store price on 2026-09-20: $79.95.
- Scents: 100ml, bible price $39.95, COGS $10, "lasts 30 days regular use." Live store price on 2026-09-20: $49.95. 50ml at $29.95 planned (about 15 days). US supplier, 21-day lead time, aluminum bottles.
- Kits (creative brief July 2026): Studio 1+1 $89.95, Condo 3+3 $189.95, House 5+5 $289.95. Live store has "Home Diffuser Kits" from $79.95 and several unlisted kit variants.
- Other live SKUs: "Priority & Full Protection Pass" $4.95 (unlisted), "Replacement Parts" (unlisted, $0), an archived "Six Month Transformation Program" product created Aug 2026.

## The locked offer stack (bible; "no other offers exist")
- 1-year diffuser warranty. 90-day money back on scents. Free shipping. Free sample booklet in every order.
- Subscription: 25% off scents when subscribed every 30 days. Swap scents anytime. Cancel anytime.
- Acquisition offer (creative brief): 30 days of fragrance free with every diffuser purchase.
- Diffuser is never discounted. Never a loss leader ("no free diffuser with subscription" plays). Never compete on price, no flash sales, no fake urgency.
- Never medical or therapeutic claims. Never emojis, em dashes, hype language, "it's not X it's Y" framing.

## Channel roles (bible)
- Shopify is the subscription engine and hero commerce. Amazon is discovery and trust, funnels to Shopify subscription via sample booklet card. TikTok Shop for creator commerce. Meta primary paid acquisition. Klaviyo email and SMS for retention, subscription nurture, win-back.
- Bible says subscription must be the most prominent purchase option on the product page and retargeting should push subscription specifically.

## Implications the report should respect
- Retention design can be built in from day one because the subscription is not live yet.
- Any recommended save offer must fit the locked offer stack or be flagged explicitly as a proposed addition to the stack (for example a pause, a cadence change, a swap, a free 50ml, or a loyalty gift is a proposal; a percentage discount deeper than 25% conflicts with "never compete on price").
- Voice constraints apply to all customer-facing copy examples: calm, warm, no urgency, no emojis, no em dashes.
