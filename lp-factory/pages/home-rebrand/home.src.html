Questions? Answered.

Can I leave it on when I go to bed?{{i:plus}}

Yes. There is no flame, no water and no heat. Set the timer for 1, 3, 6 or 12 hours and it stops on its own. The night light switches on or off separately.

Is it safe around kids and pets?{{i:plus}}

There is no flame, no smoke, no heat and no water, so it is safe to leave on overnight, in a kid’s room or near pets. Start on the subtle setting in the room where your pet sleeps.

How long does one bottle last?{{i:plus}}

About 15 to 21 days on the everyday setting. Higher settings use it faster.

Am I signing up for a subscription?{{i:plus}}

Only if you want one. When you build your kit you pick refills every 30, 60 or 90 days, or no refills at all. With refills, your scents come again for $29.95 each instead of $39.95, and we email you before every charge. Skip a delivery, swap scents or cancel any time.

How long does delivery take?{{i:plus}}

About 5 business days. Every order ships free from Miami.
