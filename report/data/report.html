Renewal recovery model — is the front-end investment worth it?

The verdict, in plain terms

Almost, but not yet — every subscriber costs $143 to acquire and returns about $136 of renewal profit within 180 days. That's $8 lost per subscriber (0.95× your money back).

The machine starts printing money when any one of these happens:

  • CAC drops below $136 per subscriber (currently $143 — needs 5% cheaper), or
  • retention beats 79% staying 180 days (you assumed 75%), or
  • subscribers keep renewing past 180 days — every extra month after that is $34/subscriber of nearly pure profit.

The first real renewal wave bills next week — those numbers will tell us if the 75% assumption holds. Until then this is a model, not a promise.

How to read this — you have invested $25,731 so far (everything spent minus everything earned). “If ads stop”, the 226 subscribers you already own keep renewing and pay back 114% of that within 180 days. “If ads continue” at today’s pace (1.4 new subscribers/day, $181/day front-end loss), the total hole grows for a while — but renewals stack until around day 7, when a normal day becomes profitable. Assumptions: 75% of subscribers renew on schedule through day 180 (the rest never renew), renewals at plan list price, renewals ship scents only ($9 per scent + $7.50 shipping + $1.00 handling) plus card fees, nothing counted past each subscriber’s 180th day.

Methodology — Net sales are product revenue after discounts, before tax; taxes are excluded as pass-through. Refunds are booked on the day issued and do not credit COGS back. COGS uses Shopify line-item unit costs. Payment fees are the actual per-transaction fees from Shopify Payments (2.9% + $0.30 estimated when an order has no fee record), plus $1.00/order handling. Kit lines carry the kit's all-inclusive cost (1 Diffuser $26 · 2+2 $70 · 3+3 $105); scents included in a kit add no cost. $0 orders (creator samples, replacements) are excluded entirely. Shipping cost is a flat estimate per order — Shopify’s API does not expose purchased label costs; replace the estimate in report/config.json when the real average is known. Blended ROAS = net sales ÷ Meta spend (all revenue, not just attributed). Meta-reported ROAS uses Meta pixel attribution. Subscription MRR normalizes every Subi contract to a 30-day month.
