The new cart.

This is the drawer that opens when someone adds to cart. It works: tap the sizes, the plus and minus, the scents. Pick a cart below to see each case.

What is in it.

  • Kit size as three steps. The only real saving in your price list is the second and third diffuser ($159 instead of $178, $239 instead of $267). The steps sit at the top and one tap changes the kit.
  • The free scent is a slot to fill. Add a diffuser and the cart asks which scent comes with it. A free thing left unclaimed is the strongest pull in a cart.
  • FREE and the struck price on every line. The buyer sees $39.95 crossed out next to each scent, and one green line with the total saved.
  • Refills are offered, never ticked for them. In your survey, the subscription was one of the three things that almost stopped buyers (6 of 23). It says the price, that you email before every charge, and that they can cancel.
  • Add a scent, picked from real orders. Euphoric Bloom and Midnight Sensation were bought together in 62 orders this year, Golden Blossom and Midnight in 51. The cart suggests the partner of what is in it, then your best sellers.
  • One real customer line and your rating, right above the button: 4.7 from 124 reviews.
  • Three promises by the button: free shipping from Miami, 30 days or every dollar back, lifetime warranty.
  • The pay bar stays on screen: what you save, free shipping, and the total inside the button, with Shopify's own Shop Pay installments line under it.
  • The empty cart still sells: the two doors from the home page and your three best sellers.

How it connects.

  • Products and prices come live from Shopify's cart, so the kit, the free-scent discounts and the refill plans show exactly what checkout will charge. Nothing is typed in by hand.
  • Add, change and remove use Shopify's own cart, the same one your kits page uses today.
  • It opens from the cart icon in the header and from every add to cart button on the theme.
  • Pixels keep working as they do now. Shopify sends the add to cart and checkout events itself, so Meta, Triple Whale, Klaviyo and Postscript receive them. Adds made inside the drawer count too.
  • The button goes to Shopify checkout, with the discounts already applied.
  • Not touched: Subi, Postscript, One Click Upsell and Gorgias settings.
  • The old drawer is removed from the theme copy you are about to publish, not hidden.

Left out on purpose.

  • No countdown and no "only 3 left". They would not be true, and your buyers are mostly women over 45 who read carefully.
  • No free shipping bar. Every order already ships free, so there is nothing to unlock. The kit steps do that job with a real saving.
  • No discount code box. It sends people off to hunt for a code. Codes still work at checkout.
  • No paid shipping protection. Two of the brands add a small charge for it by default. It would sit badly next to "free shipping".

Yours to decide.

  • Refills with a scents-only cart. Today your refill plans take $39.95 off the first payment, so a buyer with no kit who picks refills pays $0 for the first bottle. In this proposal the cart offers refills only when a kit is in it. Tell me if scents-only buyers should get the offer too.
  • The kits page. Its button goes straight to checkout today. It can open this drawer first, which gives the add-a-scent and refill offers one more chance, at the cost of one more tap.
  • Fast-pay buttons (Shop Pay, Apple Pay) under the main button. Faster for returning buyers, but they skip past the offers in the cart.
  • The full cart page at /cart. I would send it to this drawer so there is one cart to keep right.
  • Scent names. The store still calls them "Golden Blossom Harmony – Love (100ml)". The drawer can show the short names from the new pages.
