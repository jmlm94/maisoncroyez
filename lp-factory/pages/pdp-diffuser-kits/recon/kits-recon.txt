Questions?

We've got answers.

How long does the fragrance last?

Each 100ml bottle lasts weeks — not hours. Our fragrance-saving technology preserves scent over time, delivering 10x the longevity of traditional plug-ins or candles.

Does it actually fill the room?

Within minutes. Unlike candles that barely scent the corner they sit in, the Maison Croyez diffuser distributes fragrance evenly across the entire room. Soft enough to feel elegant, present enough that every guest will notice.

Is it safe for pets and kids?

Absolutely. Every fragrance is made with 100% organic ingredients from France, is hypoallergenic, and completely non-toxic. Safe for your family, your pets, and your peace of mind.

Do I need an app, WiFi, or batteries?

No, no, and no. Plug it in. That’s it. No water to refill, no app to download, no WiFi to connect. Designed for women who love elegance, not effort.

Will the diffuser look good in my home?

It was designed to. The sleek, minimalist body blends with any décor — modern, mid-century, farmhouse. No ugly cords, no plastic eyesore. It looks like a design piece and elevates your space at the same time.

What makes this different from candles?

Candles release soot, smoke, and VOCs. They barely fill a room. They burn out in hours and cost money to replace every week. Our diffuser is flame-free, fills rooms corner to corner, and a single fragrance bottle lasts for weeks. The clean, long-lasting upgrade your home deserves.

What if I don't like the scent?

Every kit comes with a 90-day risk-free trial. If you’re not completely in love, we’ll make it right. No questions, no hassle, no loose strings.

What do the "intentions" mean?

Every Maison Croyez fragrance is designed around a specific energy. Chilled Citrus is for clarity and focus. Euphoric Bloom is for love. Golden Blossom Harmony is for abundance. When you choose a scent, you’re choosing the energy you want more of in your space. Your home isn’t just a place — it’s your sanctuary.

What room should I use it in?

Any room you want to transform. Bedroom, living room, bathroom, entryway, home office — works beautifully everywhere. The Studio Kit is perfect for one key room. The House Kit fills your entire home with different intentions.

What's included in each kit?

Studio Kit ($89.95): 1 diffuser + 1 fragrance. Condo Kit ($189.95): 3 diffusers. House Kit ($289.95): 5 diffusers + 5 fragrances — best value. All kits include free shipping, lifetime warranty, and 90-day risk-free trial.
