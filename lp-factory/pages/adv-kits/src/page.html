5 reasons women are throwing out their candles for this waterless diffuser.

Soot on the ceiling, mold in the water tank, plug-ins that fade in a week. Here's what is really filling the air at home, and the hotel-style fix that is free right now.

By the Maison Croyez editorsUpdated October 29, 2026

You light a candle so the house feels like home. You keep a plug-in by the front door. Maybe you bought one of those water diffusers that puffs out a little cloud.

It all smells nice for an hour. But each one has a cost most of us never think about, and it ends up on your ceiling, in your water tank, and in the air you breathe all evening.

Here's what so many women are switching to instead, and why.

1

Every candle puts smoke in the room.

The black ring inside the jar and the grey smudge above the shelf are soot. A flame makes it every time it burns, and it doesn't stay in the jar. It drifts through the room you're sitting in.

Then there's the flame itself. Candles start thousands of home fires in the U.S. every year, according to the National Fire Protection Association. You can't leave one burning when you go to bed or step out.

✓The swap: a waterless diffuser has no flame and makes no smoke. Leave it on overnight, in a bedroom, in a kid's room or near pets.
2

The water diffuser is growing something.

Water diffusers sit full of water for days. A warm tank of standing water is where mold and bacteria grow, and whatever grows in the tank gets misted into the room along with your oil.

Most of us rinse it when we remember. Few of us remember.

✓The swap: no water and no tank. Nothing to grow, nothing to scrub, nothing to spill.

“I can go to sleep with it on in our bedroom and feel safe. Also love how quickly the scent fills the room.”

Maison Croyez customer survey · bought it for safety and no mold
3

Plug-ins, sprays and reed sticks only scent the corner they sit in.

A candle, a plug-in and a reed stick all work the same way. They warm or wick the scent so it evaporates right next to the device. That's why you smell them from four feet away and nowhere else.

It's also why a plug-in is strong for a week, then fades into the wallpaper, and why a spray covers a smell for a few minutes and then it's back.
