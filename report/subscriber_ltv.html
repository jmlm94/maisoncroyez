Read-out

  • The first order does not pay for anything. The average subscriber pays $80 up front and leaves $5 after the diffuser, scent, shipping and fees. Cohorts on the $39.95 single (Jul 20, Jul 27, Aug 17) start at zero or slightly negative.
  • Each paid renewal is worth about $57 of revenue and $30 to $35 of contribution. A customer on their 2nd order has produced $36 of contribution on average (23% of the cost to acquire them); on their 3rd order, $42 (27%).
  • Payback needs 5 to 6 paid renewals at today's costs and prices. The Jul 13 launch cohort, the only one two renewals deep, has returned $33 per subscriber against a $295 acquisition cost.
  • The build-your-kit cohorts (Aug 10, Aug 17) have the best LTV so far: $36 to $41 of contribution per subscriber through renewal 1, because they carry more scents per delivery and renew at a higher rate, and they were bought at $87 to $125.
  • 43 customers were due a renewal and never paid one. They generated $10,341 of revenue in total and are the group where payment recovery would move LTV the most.
The LTV number to plan with today: about $23 of contribution per subscriber to date, heading toward roughly $60 to $70 once the August cohorts complete their second renewal if retention holds at 60% to 70%. At $22.50/$4.50 costs and 80% recovery, the same subscriber reaches $100+ within three cycles and the $87 acquisition weeks become profitable inside a quarter.

Data as of September 25, 2026. Contribution = order value − product cost (diffuser $26, scent $9, kit costs per the runbook) − $7.50 shipping − processing fees − $1 handling. Ad cost is not deducted; it is shown as cost per subscriber.
